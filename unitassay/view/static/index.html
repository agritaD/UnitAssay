Layer 1 (isolate) splits the recording into frames and, in each, measures the L-ratio of the unit against its reference ("noise") channel and against every neighbour on its peak channel. A frame is clean when every L-ratio is below the threshold, and absent frames count as not clean. Layer 2 (curate) uses those numbers with the dip test, ISI violations and pair CCGs to decide PASS / FAIL / LOW_SPIKE_COUNT. The rule shown is the one that decided.
